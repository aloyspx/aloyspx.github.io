🔍 Biography
======

I'm a PhD student in Computer Engineering at Polytechnique Montréal, working on machine learning for medical image analysis. I focus on multi-task and self-supervised learning to build models that generalize across domains and reduce the annotation burden for clinicians. I'm also passionate about medical device innovation and entrepreneurship. Outside the lab, you’ll probably find me at the hockey rink 🏒!
