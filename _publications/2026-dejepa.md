DeJEPA learns voxel-level representations from unlabelled volumetric medical images. It combines joint-embedding prediction, a multi-scale 3D backbone, and distribution regularization without requiring negative pairs.

The work evaluates the learned features on BTCV and Medical Segmentation Decathlon tasks, including probing and fine-tuning with limited labelled data.
