This study develops and externally tests an nnU-Net model for three related findings on non-contrast head CT: intracerebral hemorrhage, intraventricular hemorrhage, and perihematomal edema. The work combines expert annotations with evaluation on an independent clinical cohort.

My contribution included developing and validating baseline segmentation models and contributing to manuscript preparation. The article describes a planned public release of the dataset and model; see the publisher's page for availability.
