This conference abstract describes a 3D Slicer extension that uses nnU-Net to support expert-supervised volumetric monitoring of pediatric low-grade glioma across serial MRI scans. It explores patient-specific longitudinal assessment and the practical workflow for reviewing segmentations.

Presented at the 22nd International Symposium on Pediatric Neuro-Oncology (ISPNO 2026) and published in the conference supplement of *Neuro-Oncology Pediatrics*.
